## Commits

Use [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/en/v1.0.0/): `<type>(<optional scope>): <summary>`.

- Types: `feat`, `fix`, `refactor`, `perf`, `test`, `docs`, `build`, `chore`.
- Scope is the area touched, e.g. `sync`, `store`, `mime`, `reader`, `ui`.
- Summary in the imperative, lower case, no trailing period, under 72 characters.
- Breaking changes: `!` after the type/scope and a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.
